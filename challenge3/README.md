### Algorithm

1. Start.

2. Input purchase amount.

3. Input customer type.

4. Input delivery distance.

5. If the customer is a student and the purchase amount is ₹2000 or more, give a 15% discount; otherwise, no discount.

6. Calculate the discount amount.

7. Calculate the amount after discount.

8. If distance is up to 5 km, delivery charge is ₹30.

9. If distance is up to 15 km, delivery charge is ₹50; otherwise, ₹100.

10. Calculate the final payable amount.

11. Display the bill details.

12. Stop.
